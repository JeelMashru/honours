The AI Team (System Architecture)

Who was just talking in the terminal above? Here is the 4-agent network.

The Planner

Organizes the workflow and breaks down the main problem into smaller, manageable tasks.

The Verifier

Audits the data against historical records to catch math errors or suspicious entries.

The Negotiator

Communicates with external databases to figure out exactly why the data isn't matching.

The Recoverer

Writes and deploys code patches to fix the root cause of the error automatically.

The Problem

Relying on a Single AI Model

Using just one AI model for complex financial tasks can be risky. A single AI can make math mistakes (hallucinations) or get confused during long tasks, and it usually can't double-check its own work.

Our Team-Based Solution

Instead of one AI doing everything, we split the job across a team of specialized AI agents. Because they check each other's work, the system catches errors immediately, making it much safer for handling financial data.
